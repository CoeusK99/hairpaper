## 3. Kerion-like scalp disease in a child with a culture isolate reported as.

*Medical mycology case reports* · 2026-10-31 · `落髮 / 雄性禿、生髮 / 毛囊再生`
> Wang F, Ding W

A 7-year-old boy presented with kerion-like inflammatory alopecia. Direct microscopy demonstrated fungal hyphae, and a cultured mould was reported asby mass spectrometry. Treatment comprised oral terbinafine for 3 months and oral prednisone for 2 months. Fungal examination was negative and Wood lamp examination was normal at 3 months; hair regrowth was gradually recovering at the 6-month follow-up. This case illustrates the need to interpret an unusual mould isolate alongside clinical findings before assigning causation.
