## 1. Biologics in alopecia areata: promise, paradox, and the precision medicine frontier.

*Expert opinion on biological therapy* · 2026-10-21 · `落髮 / 雄性禿`
> Tang Z, Wu J, Li C, Wang R

INTRODUCTION: Alopecia areata (AA) is a chronic, immune-mediated non-scarring hair loss affecting approximately 2% of the population. The approval of oral JAK inhibitors has established targeted systemic therapy for moderate-to-severe AA. Incomplete response rates, relapse upon treatment discontinuation, and safety contraindications, however, leave a substantial proportion of patients requiring alternative options.

AREAS COVERED: This review evaluates the immunopathogenic rationale and available clinical evidence for biologic therapies in AA, including inhibitors of tumor necrosis factor (TNF), interleukin-4 receptor α subunit (IL-4 Rα), IL-17, and IL-23.
