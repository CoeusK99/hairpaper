Folliculitis decalvans (FD) is a chronic neutrophilic cicatricial alopecia characterized by recurrent scalp inflammation, pustules, tufted hairs, and progressive permanent hair loss. This systematic review summarizes available treatment approaches and reported outcomes in FD.
