## 5. [Fibrosing alopecia in a pattern distribution-recognition, differential diagnosis, and treatment].

*Dermatologie (Heidelberg, Germany)* · 2026-11-24 · `落髮 / 雄性禿`
> Eren S, Beyer K, Olejarczyk-Ekman I

Fibrosing alopecia in a pattern distribution (FAPD) is a lymphocytic scarring alopecia that can clinically mimic androgenetic alopecia (AGA). Trichoscopy is therefore important in AGA-like hair loss to identify features suggestive of an inflammatory scarring follicular process. Correlation of clinical, trichoscopic and histopathological findings plays a central role in the diagnostic assessment. Early recognition is therapeutically important because treatment directed at AGA alone does not address the inflammatory scarring component. This may allow scarring to progress and result in irreversible follicular loss.

## 6. [Alopecia areata: recognise, assess, treat].

*Dermatologie (Heidelberg, Germany)* · 2026-11-24 · `落髮 / 雄性禿`
> Beyer K, Olejarczyk-Ekman I, Eren S

Alopecia areata (AA) is an immune-mediated, nonscarring hair disorder with collapse of follicular immune privilege. Diagnosis is based on clinical examination and trichoscopy. Preserved follicular ostia distinguish it from scarring alopecias. The Severity of Alopecia Tool (SALT) score quantifies scalp hair loss, but not activity or burden-both require separate assessment. JAK inhibitors are the first approved systemic therapies. Response follows the hair cycle and can only be judged after 3-6 months. The following apply for children and adolescents: separate algorithms, evidence and approvals are limited, and families should be included in care.
