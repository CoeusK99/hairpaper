OBJECTIVES: To characterise acute RIA in dogs with nasal tumours and to evaluate associations with coat type and radiation dose.

MATERIALS AND METHODS: Medical records were reviewed retrospectively. Dogs were classified by coat type (straight versus curly or wire-haired).
