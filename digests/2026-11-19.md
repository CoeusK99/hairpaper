## 5. Fibrosing Alopecia in a Pattern Distribution in the Pediatric Population: A Potentially Underdiagnosed Entity.

*Pediatric dermatology* · 2026-11-17 · `落髮 / 雄性禿`
> Cote M, Lawrence CN, Lo Sicco KI, Weiler N, Oza VS, Occidental M, Orlow SJ

Fibrosing alopecia in a pattern distribution (FAPD) is an uncommon scarring alopecia with features of androgenetic alopecia (AGA) and lichen planopilaris (LPP); pediatric disease is poorly characterized. We report three male patients aged 12-16 years with patterned hair loss, dermoscopic miniaturization/follicular dropout, and histopathology showing perifollicular lymphocytic inflammation and fibrosis, fulfilling FAPD criteria. This case series highlights the need for early histopathologic confirmation to enable timely treatment and prevent irreversible scarring in pediatric FAPD.
